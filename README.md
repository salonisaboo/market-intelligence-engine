## 📊 Project Description — Market Intelligence Engine

Market Intelligence Engine is an NLP-based analytics project that converts financial news and textual market data into actionable sentiment insights. The system processes unstructured financial content, applies text preprocessing and machine learning techniques, and classifies sentiment into positive, negative, or neutral categories.

The objective of this project is to simulate a real-world decision-support tool that helps businesses and analysts understand market perception, identify trends, and make data-driven investment or strategy decisions. By transforming news sentiment into structured insights, the project demonstrates how analytics and technology can be combined to support business intelligence, risk monitoring, and strategic planning.
